I am a research associate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at [The University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/). I will begin my PhD in Autumn 2027 under the supervision of Prof. Zhongliang Jiang. I earned my Bachelor of Engineering from the [Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (Yao Class)](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on **AI for Medicine**, especially multimodal learning for medical video understanding, diagnostic report generation, and medical image analysis. I am also interested in generative computer vision and machine learning applications in science and engineering.

## Research experience

- **A Self-Reflective Iterative Framework for Ultrasound Video Understanding:** my current work with Prof. Zhongliang Jiang in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The University of Hong Kong.
- **DINO-ECHO:** multimodal echocardiography video interpretation and automated report generation, advised by Prof. Sheng Wang at the University of Washington. [Project repository](https://github.com/xy-liang22/DINO-ECHO)

## Interests beyond research

I enjoy music, dance, choral performance and sports. I am previously a member of the Tsinghua University student chorus.
